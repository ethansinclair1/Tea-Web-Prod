Why small batch

We buy less, so we can taste all of it first.

Amber Hour orders in small lots directly tied to named gardens and small processors, rather than blended commodity stock. That means a bag can sell out and not come back until the next harvest — we'd rather tell you that plainly than pad the shelf with something we wouldn't steep ourselves.

Every tea on this page is one we drink at home. If a lot doesn't taste right, it doesn't go up for sale.
