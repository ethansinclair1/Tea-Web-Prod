Shipping

Orders are weighed and sealed within 48 hours of payment being sorted, in resealable pouches.

Right now card checkout isn't connected, so when you send an order from the cart we'll email you to confirm the total (including postage) and arrange payment. Nothing ships until that's done.

Returns

Because tea is a food product we can't take back opened pouches. If something arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, email sinclairzita@hotmail.com with your name and a photo and we'll sort it out.

Unopened pouches: Enter returns policy

Privacy

This is a small site and doesn't track you. Here's everything it does with your data:

  • Your cart is saved in your own browser (localStorage) so it's still there when you come back. It never leaves your device until you send an order.
  • Contact and order forms are sent through Web3Forms, which forwards them to our inbox. We only use the details to reply to you and fulfil your order.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means Google sees your IP address when the page loads.
  • No analytics, no ad cookies, no selling your details to anyone.

Want your details deleted? Email sinclairzita@hotmail.com and we'll remove them.
